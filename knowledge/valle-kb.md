## 18. OUR STORY (for brand/tone questions)

Vallé began as a family tea plantation in the south of Mauritius. The owner discovered the 23 hues of
Mauritian soil on the land, and over the years transformed the plantation into an adventure park —
building a quad trail around a natural waterfall path, and ziplines to take in the ocean views.
Nature was preserved and integrated rather than replaced; wildlife roams freely as part of the ecosystem.
Tagline: **"Your Advenature Begins Here"** / *"Feel the pulse of Mauritius."*

## 19. OUR STORY IN DETAIL (from the Vallé Magazine & Factsheet)

Decades ago, a young father stepped onto his family's land in the heart of Mauritius: a tea plantation.
Surveying the valley he noticed the earth itself was alive with colour and discovered the **23 hues of
Mauritian soil**. Year after year he transformed the land: a natural pathway to a waterfall became a quad
trail; stunning ocean views inspired ziplines that let people soar. Wildlife roamed freely and was
nurtured as part of the ecosystem: he didn't just build, he preserved and integrated nature into every
experience. Today Vallé stands as a testament to that vision.

**Founder's message:** "Never say adventure is impossible, make it unforgettable!" Vallé is "where
adventure meets the soul of Mauritius": a place of connection, born of nature and made for adventure.
Its purpose: to unearth the true colours of Mauritius and invite guests to experience them not as
visitors but as part of the story.

**Vallé is recognised by the World Luxury Travel Awards.**

## 20. THE SPIRIT OF ADVENATURE — HIGH PULSE & LOW PULSE

*Advenature* (adventure + nature) is Vallé's way of life, lived in two rhythms:
- **High Pulse** — heart-racing moments: ziplines over the valleys, quad and buggy trails, the Bicycle
  Zipline, the Luge Kart, the Nepalese Bridge.
- **Low Pulse** — time slows down: wandering the forests and Rock Garden, breathing by a hidden
  waterfall, the 23 Coloured Earth, The Peak, dining by the water, meeting the wildlife.
The spirit invites guests to embrace both the rush and the calm: "Feel the pulse of Mauritius, one
pulse at a time."

## 21. NATURE, FLORA & FAUNA

- **23 Coloured Earth** — a rare geological phenomenon formed by volcanic ash and mineral-rich
  elements: deep reds, browns, golden yellows, purples, blues and greens found nowhere else on earth.
- **Flora** — over 50 species of endemic trees, including Black Ebony and Bois de Natte.
- **Fauna** — wild Java deer grazing freely, giant tortoises, rare albino deer, tropical birds; and
  Souris, the friendly domesticated deer at Le Palmiste.
- **Waterfalls** — Vacoas, Chamouzé and Cheveux d'Ange.
- **Rock Garden** — inspired by Zen gardens: rock formations, flowers and plant life for reflection.
- **The Peak** — highest point of the southern mountains, reached by luxury 4x4 or safari car through
  Java-deer wilderness; views from Mahebourg's coastline to Le Morne.
- **Viewpoints** — La Tour viewpoint and several lookouts over the Savanne mountains and south coast.

## 30. HOW LONG TO ALLOW FOR A VISIT

Guests often ask how much time they need. Answer from the activity durations rather
than inventing a figure:

- **One activity plus the walking trail:** roughly 2 to 3 hours.
  (Luge 15 min · Bicycle Zipline 15 min · Nepalese Bridge 20–25 min · Waterfall
  Zipline 20 min · quad or buggy 1 h · Discovery/Sky Pulse ziplines 1 h–1 h 15.)
- **A package (admission, two or three activities and lunch):** half to a full day.
- **The flagship Advenature Flight alone** is 2 h to 2 h 30, and the Advenature quad
  tour is 2 h; combine either with lunch and a walk and the day is full.
- **A relaxed family day** with several activities, lunch and time at the Kids Park,
  the Rock Garden and the 23 Coloured Earth: plan most of the day, arriving early.

The park is open daily 09:00–17:30; arriving in the morning leaves the most choice.
